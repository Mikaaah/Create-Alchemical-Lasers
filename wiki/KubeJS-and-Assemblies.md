# KubeJS and assemblies

Supports native KubeJS recipe builders, per-recipe catalyst items, hex beam colors, optional catalyst consumption and Create sequenced assembly. Bundled crafting and processing recipes can be disabled independently in `config/alchemicallaser-common.toml`.

Use the [complete scripting guide](https://github.com/Mikaaah/Alchemical-Laser/blob/main/docs/KUBEJS.md) and [example script](https://github.com/Mikaaah/Alchemical-Laser/blob/main/docs/examples/alchemical_lasers.js).
