# Getting started

Requires Java 21, NeoForge 21.1.219+ within 21.1.x and Create 6.0.10 (6.0.x), with Create’s dependencies. JEI, EMI and KubeJS are optional.

Place an emitter over a belt or Depot with one air block between them. Connect the horizontal shaft, supply at least 16 RPM, insert a matching lens or catalyst and feed the recipe input. Redstone power disables processing. See the [README](https://github.com/Mikaaah/Create-Alchemical-Lasers#how-it-works) for setup and migration details.
